# Objetos Mágicos (DMG)

Los objetos mágicos se obtienen de los tesoros de monstruos derrotados o se descubren en bóvedas perdidas hace mucho tiempo. Estos objetos otorgan capacidades que un personaje rara vez podría tener de otro modo, o complementan las capacidades de su dueño de maneras asombrosas.

### Armadura

Un objeto en la categoría de Armadura suele ser una versión mágica de una armadura de la sección "Equipo". A menos que la descripción de una armadura indique lo contrario, la armadura debe llevarse puesta para que su magia funcione.

Algunas armaduras mágicas especifican el tipo de armadura que son, como una cota de malla o una armadura de placas. Si no se especifica ningún tipo, elige el tipo o determínalo al azar.

### Pociones

Un objeto en la categoría de Poción puede ser un brebaje mágico que debe ser ingerido o un aceite que debe aplicarse a una criatura o un objeto. Una poción típica consiste en 30 ml (1 onza) de líquido en un vial.

_Usar una poción._ Las pociones son objetos consumibles. Beber una poción o administrársela a otra criatura requiere una **Acción Adicional**. Aplicar un aceite puede requerir más tiempo, según se indique en su descripción. Una vez usada, la poción surte efecto inmediatamente y se agota.

_Mezclar pociones._ Un personaje puede beber una poción mientras aún está bajo los efectos de otra o verter varias pociones en un solo recipiente. Los extraños ingredientes utilizados para crear pociones pueden provocar interacciones impredecibles.

Cuando un personaje mezcla dos pociones, tira en la tabla Mezcla de Pociones. Si se combinan más de dos, tira de nuevo por cada poción adicional, combinando los resultados. A menos que los efectos sean inmediatamente obvios, revélalos solo cuando se hagan evidentes.

### Anillos

Para que su magia funcione, un objeto en la categoría de Anillo debe llevarse puesto en un dedo u apéndice similar, a menos que su descripción indique lo contrario.

### Varas

Un objeto en la categoría de Vara es un cetro generalmente hecho de metal, madera o hueso. Una vara típica pesa entre 1 y 2,5 kg. A menos que su descripción indique lo contrario, una vara puede utilizarse como un canalizador arcano.

### Pergaminos

Un objeto en la categoría de Pergamino es un rollo de papel, pergamino o vitela que contiene una fórmula mágica o un conjuro. Un pergamino típico mide unos 20 por 30 cm.

_Usar un pergamino._ Lanzar el conjuro desde un pergamino requiere el tiempo de lanzamiento normal del conjuro. Una vez lanzado el conjuro, el pergamino se desmorona en polvo. Si el conjuro se interrumpe, el pergamino no se consume.

### Bastones

Un objeto en la categoría de Bastón mide entre 1,5 y 1,8 m de largo y suele estar hecho de madera, metal o hueso. A menos que su descripción indique lo contrario, un bastón puede empuñarse como un bastón de combate y utilizarse como canalizador arcano o druídico.

### Varitas

Un objeto en la categoría de Varita mide entre 30 y 45 cm de largo y suele estar hecho de madera, hueso, metal o cristal. Una varita típica pesa unos 250 g. A menos que su descripción indique lo contrario, una varita puede usarse como canalizador arcano.

### Armas

Un objeto en la categoría de Arma suele ser una versión mágica de un arma de la sección "Equipo".

### Objetos Maravillosos

Los objetos maravillosos son la categoría más diversa de objetos mágicos, e incluyen prendas de vestir, libros, joyas y objetos utilitarios que no encajan en las otras categorías.

## Reglas de los Objetos Mágicos

### Rareza de los Objetos Mágicos

Cada objeto mágico tiene una rareza: Común, Poco común, Raro, Muy raro, Legendario o Artefacto.

**Valor de los Objetos Mágicos por Rareza**
| Rareza | Nivel de personaje sugerido | Valor de mercado aproximado |
|---|---|---|
| Común | 1 o superior | 50–100 po |
| Poco común | 1 o superior | 101–500 po |
| Raro | 5 o superior | 501–5000 po |
| Muy raro | 11 o superior | 5001–50 000 po |
| Legendario | 17 o superior | 50 001+ po |
| Artefacto | 17 o superior | Incalculable |

### Activar un Objeto Mágico

Activar un objeto mágico suele requerir una acción (como una **Acción de magia**, **Acción adicional** o una **Palabra de mando**). Si el objeto permite lanzar un conjuro, el lanzamiento no requiere componentes a menos que la descripción indique lo contrario.

### Cargas

Muchos objetos mágicos tienen cargas que se gastan para activar sus propiedades. Al sintonizarse con el objeto o mediante el conjuro *identificar*, el personaje conoce cuántas cargas tiene y cuántas recupera cada amanecer. Si se gasta la última carga, suele tirarse 1d20: con un 1, el objeto pierde su magia o queda destruido.

### Objetos Malditos

Algunos objetos mágicos albergan maldiciones. Identificar un objeto no suele revelar una maldición. La sintonización con un objeto maldito solo puede romperse mediante un conjuro de *levantar maldición* u otra magia similar.

### Objetos Mágicos Inteligentes

Algunos objetos mágicos poseen consciencia propia, con puntuaciones de Inteligencia, Sabiduría y Carisma, alineamiento, sentidos y formas de comunicación (empatía, telepatía o voz hablada). En situaciones de desacuerdo ético o moral, un objeto inteligente puede entrar en conflicto con su portador mediante una prueba enfrentada de Carisma.

## Objetos Mágicos A–Z

Magic items are presented in alphabetical order.

If a magic item description capitalizes a creature's name and presents it in **bold** type, that's a visual cue pointing you to the creature's stat block. Unless the text states otherwise, the stat block is in "Monsters." How to read and use a stat block is explained in "Monsters."

#### Armadura de adamantina

_Armor (Any Medium or Heavy, Except Hide Armadura), Uncommon_

This suit of Armadura is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit.

#### Munición, +1, +2 o +3

_Weapon (Any Ammunition), poco común (+1), raro (+2), or Very raro (+3)_

You have a bonus to tirada de ataques and tiradas de daño made with this piece of magic ammunition. The bonus is determined by the rarity of the ammunition. Una vez it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

This ammunition is typically found or sold in quantities of ten or twenty pieces. Ten pieces of this ammunition are equivalent in value to a Poción of the same rarity.

#### Munición de matanza

_Weapon (Any Ammunition), Very Rare_

This magic ammunition is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the GM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below. If a creature of that type takes damage from the ammunition, the creature makes a DC 17 Constitution tirada de salvación, taking an extra 6d10 Force damage on a failed save or half as much extra damage on a successful one.

After dealing its extra damage to a creature, the ammunition becomes nonmagical.

#### Escudo animado

_Armor (Escudo), Very raro (requiere sintonía)_

While holding this Escudo, you can take a Acción adicional to cause it to animate. The Escudo leaps into the air and hovers in your space to protect you as if you were wielding it, leaving your hands free. The Escudo remains animate for 1 minute, until you take a Acción adicional to end this effect, or until you die or have the Incapacitated condition, at which point the Escudo falls to the ground or into your hand if you have one free.

#### Aparato del cangrejo

_Wondrous Item, Legendary_

This item first appears to be a sealed iron barrel weighing 225 kg. The barrel has a hidden catch, which can be found with a successful DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check. Releasing the catch unlocks a hatch at one end of the barrel, allowing two Medium or smaller creatures to crawl inside. Ten levers are set in a row at the far end, each in a neutral position, able to move up or down. When certain levers are used, the apparatus transforms to resemble a giant lobster.

The _Apparatus of the Crab_ is a Large object with the following statistics: AC 20; HP 200; Speed 30 pies, Swim 30 pies (or 0 pies for both if the legs aren't extended); Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage.

To be used as a vehicle, the apparatus requires one pilot. While the apparatus's hatch is closed, the compartment is airtight and watertight. The compartment holds enough air for 10 hours of breathing, divided by the number of breathing creatures inside.

The apparatus floats on water. It can also go underwater to a depth of 900 pies. Below that, the vehicle takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage each minute from pressure.

A creature in the compartment can take a Utilize action to move as muchos as two of the apparatus's levers up or down. After each use, a lever goes back to its neutral position. Each lever, from left to right, functions as shown in the Apparatus of the Crab Levers table.

_Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), raro (requiere sintonía)_

#### Bolsa devoradora

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare_

This bag resembles a _Bag of Holding_ but is a feeding orifice for a gigantic extradimensional creature. Turning the bag inside out closes the orifice.

The extradimensional creature attached to the bag can sense whatever is placed inside the bag. Animal or vegetable matter placed wholly in the bag is devoured and lost forever. When part of a living creature is placed in the bag, as happens when someone reaches inside it, there is a 50 percent chance that the creature is pulled inside the bag. **Golden Lions (raro).** These gold statuettes of lions are always created in pairs. You can use one figurine or both simultaneously. Each can become a **Lion** for up to 1 hour. Una vez a lion has been used, it can't be used again until 7 days have passed.

**Ivory Goats (raro).** These ivory statuettes of goats are always created in sets of three. Each goat looks unique and functions differently from the others. Their properties are as follows:

**Goat of Terror.** This figurine can become a **Giant Goat** for up to 3 hours. The goat can't attack, but you can (harmlessly) remove its horns and use them as weapons. One horn becomes a _+1 Lance_, and the other becomes a _+2 Longsword_. Removing a horn requires a acción de magia, and the weapons disappear and the horns return when the goat reverts to figurine form. While you ride the goat, any Hostile creature that starts its turn within

a 30-pies Emanation originating from the goat must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom tirada de salvación or have the Frightened condition for 1 minute, until you are no longer riding the goat, or until the goat reverts to figurine form. The Frightened creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Una vez it succeeds on the save, a creature is immune to this effect for the next 24 hours. Una vez the figurine has been used, it can't be used again until 15 days have passed.

**Goat of Traveling.** This figurine can become a Large goat with the same statistics as a **Riding Horse**. It has 24 cargas, and each hour or portion thereof it spends in goat form costs 1 carga. While it has cargas, you can use it as often as you wish. When it runs out of cargas, it reverts to a figurine and can't be used again until 7 days have passed, when it regains all expended cargas.

**Goat of Travail.** This figurine can become a Giant **Goat** for up to 3 hours. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 30 days have passed.

**Marble Elephant (raro).** This marble statuette resembles a trumpeting elephant. It can become an **Elephant** for up to 24 hours. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 7 days have passed.

**Obsidian Steed (Very raro).** This polished obsidian horse can become a **Nightmare** for up to 24 hours. The nightmare fights only to defend itself. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 5 days have passed.

The figurine has a 10 percent chance each time you use it to ignore your orders, including a command to revert to figurine form. If you mount the nightmare while it is ignoring your orders, you and the nightmare are instantly transported to a random location on the plane of Hades, where the nightmare reverts to figurine form.

**Onyx Dog (raro).** This onyx statuette of a dog can become a **Mastiff** for up to 6 hours. The mastiff has an Intelligence of 8 and can speak común. It also has Blindsight with a range of 60 pies. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 7 days have passed.

**Serpentine Owl (raro).** This serpentine statuette of an owl can become a **Giant Owl** for up to 8 hours. The owl can communicate telepathically with you at any range if you and it are on the same plane of existence. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 2 days have passed.

**Silver Raven (poco común).** This silver statuette of a raven can become a **Raven** for up to 12 hours. Una vez it has been used, it can't be used again until 2 days have passed. While in raven form, the figurine grants you the ability to cast _Animal Messenger_ on it.

_Wondrous Item, Rare_

This backpack has a central pouch and two side pouches, each of which is an extradimensional space. Each side pouch can hold up to 90 kg of material, not exceeding a volume of 25 cubic pies. The central pouch can hold up to 225 kg of material, not exceeding a volume of 64 cubic pies. The haversack always weighs 2.2 kg, regardless of its contents.

Retrieving an item from the haversack requires a Utilize action or a Acción adicional (your choice). When you reach into the haversack for a specific item, the item is always magically on top.

If any of its pouches is overloaded, pierced, or torn, the haversack ruptures and is destroyed. If the haversack is destroyed, its contents are lost forever, although an artefacto always turns up again somewhere. If the haversack is turned inside out, its contents spill forth unharmed, and the haversack must be put right before it can be used again.

Each pouch of the haversack holds enough air for 10 minutes of breathing, divided by the number of breathing creatures inside.

Placing the haversack inside an extradimensional space created by a _Bag of Holding_, _Portable Hole_, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates where the one item was placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 pies of the gate and not behind Total Cover is sucked through it and deposited in a random location on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. #### Yelmo de brillantez

_Wondrous Item, Very raro (requiere sintonía)_

a tirada de salvación against a spell. On a roll of 1, the helm emits beams of light from its remaining gems and is then destroyed. #### Fortaleza instantánea

Shrinking the tower back down to statuette form doesn't repair damage to the tower. Only a _Wish_ spell can repair the tower (this use of the spell counts as replicating a spell of level 8 or lower). Each casting of _Wish_ causes the tower to regain all its Puntos de Golpe.

#### Piedra de Ioun

_Wondrous Item, Rarity Varies (requiere sintonía)_

Roughly marble sized, _Ioun Stones_ are named after Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds. Muchos types of _Ioun Stones_ exist, each type a distinct combination of shape and color.

When you take a acción de magia to toss an _Ioun Stone_ into the air, the stone orbits your head at a distance of 1d3 pies, conferring its benefit to you while doing so. You can have up to three _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head at the same time.

Each _Ioun Stone_ orbiting your head is considered to be an object you are wearing. The orbiting stone avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to avoid collisions and thwarting all attempts by other creatures to attack or snatch it.

As a Utilize action, you can seize and stow any number of _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. If your Attunement to an _Ioun Stone_ ends while it's orbiting your head, the stone falls as though you had dropped it.

The type of stone determines its rarity and effects.

_Absorption (Very raro)_. While this pale lavender ellipsoid orbits your head, you can take a Reaction to cancel a spell of level 4 or lower cast by a creature you can see. A canceled spell has no effect, and any resources used to cast it are wasted. Una vez the stone has canceled 20 levels of spells, it burns out, turns dull gray, and loses its magic.

No object created by a pot of pigment can have a value greater than 25 GP, and the total value of all objects created by a pot of pigment can't exceed 500 GP. If you paint objects of greater value (such as a large pile of gold), they look authentic, but close inspection reveals they're made from paste, cookies, or some other worthless material.

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare_

When this 4-pies-tall, 2-pies-wide mirror is viewed indirectly, its surface shows faint images of creatures. A creature that knows the mirror's nature makes the save with Advantage, and Constructs succeed on the save automatically.

An extradimensional cell is an infinite expanse filled with thick fog that reduces visibility to 10 pies. Creatures trapped in the mirror's cells don't age, and they don't need to eat, drink, or sleep. A creature trapped within a cell can escape using magic that permits planar travel. Otherwise, the creature is confined to the cell until freed.

Placing the mirror inside an extradimensional space created by a _Bag of Holding_, _Portable Hole_, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates where the one item was placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 pies of the gate and not behind Total Cover is sucked through it to a random location on the Astral Plane. #### Vara of Absorption

_Rod, Very raro (requiere sintonía)_

While holding this Vara, you can take a Reaction to absorb a spell that is targeting only you and doesn't create an area of effect. The absorbed spell's effect is canceled, and the spell's energy—not the spell itself—is stored in the Vara. The energy has the same level as the spell when it was cast. A canceled spell dissipates with no effect, and any resources used to cast it are wasted. The Vara can absorb and store up to 50 levels of energy over the course of its existence. Una vez the Vara absorbs 50 levels of energy, it can't absorb more. If you are targeted by a spell that the Vara can't store, the Vara has no effect on that spell.

When you become attuned to the Vara, you know how muchos levels of energy the Vara has absorbed over the course of its existence and how muchos levels of spell energy it currently has stored.

If you are a spellcaster holding the Vara, you can convert energy stored in it into espacios de conjuro to cast spells you have prepared or know. You can create espacios de conjuro only of a level equal to or lower than your own espacios de conjuro, up to a maximum of level 5. You use the stored levels in place of your slots but otherwise cast the spell as normal. Por ejemplo, you can use 3 levels stored in the Vara as a level 3 espacio de conjuro.

A newly found Vara typically has 1d10 levels of spell energy stored in it. A Vara that can no longer absorb spell energy and has no energy remaining becomes nonmagical.
